Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026.

This policy explains what information the website at mabrar.me collects and how it is used. You can reach us at memrabrar@gmail.com or 03206421287.

Information you send us

When you send a project request on the Contact page, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, email address, project description, and, if you choose to give them, your phone number, company name, chosen service, budget, timeline and a reference link.

How we use it

We use this information only to read your request, reply to you and discuss your project. We do not publish it, and we do not sell it.

Where it is stored

Requests are stored in a database provided by Supabase. The website is served by Netlify. These providers process data on our behalf so the website can work. Only the site administrator can read project requests.

How long we keep it

We keep a request for as long as we need it to respond and to keep a record of our conversation. You can ask us to delete your request by writing to the email address above.

Cookies and analytics

At the time of writing, this website does not use advertising cookies or analytics. If we add analytics, we will update this page first.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us at the email address above.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date.
